## Clinic photographs

Earlier versions used genuine photographs of The LUXE Clinique taken from its Google Business
Profile, cropped and colour-graded (warmer, softer contrast, light blush wash).
A filler-brand banner with before/after faces was cropped out of the reception
photo. The clinic confirmed on 2026-11-01 that these are its own photos.

`about-room` (treatment room), `clinic-consult-room` (consultation room), and
`contact-exterior` and `home-clinic-interior` (reception), were supplied directly by the clinic on 2026-11-03.

## Original artwork

`gallery-art` is an original three.js 3D brand composition made for this project
from the clinic's own logo (emblem, wordmark and Skin · Hair · Laser · Wellness).
`about-hero` is a portrait crop of the clinic-supplied reception photo.
