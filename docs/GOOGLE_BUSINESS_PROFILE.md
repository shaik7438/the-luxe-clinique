## 1. Fix first (these affect ranking and can risk suspension)

1. **Business name.** The listing is called "The Luxe Clinique- Aesthetic and Skin Clinic in Preet Vihar, New Delhi".
   Adding keywords and a location to the name is against Google's guidelines and can get a listing suspended.
   Change it to the real-world name on the signage: **The LUXE Clinique**. The categories, description and services
   below carry the keywords instead.
2. **Monday hours.** The listing shows Monday "Closed" as a Diwali change. Confirm the regular Monday hours and use
   **Special hours** for festival closures, so the normal weekly hours stay correct.
   Regular hours now on the website: Tue–Sat 10 am–8 pm, Sun 10 am–4 pm.
3. **Second phone number.** The website also lists +91 88828 33050. If it is a live clinic line, add it as an
   additional phone on the listing; if not, tell us and it will be removed from the site.
4. **Photos.** Google requires listing photos to show the real business. Upload genuine photos of the reception,
   treatment rooms, consultation room and team. Do not upload AI-generated or heavily edited images (the reception,
   consultation-room and treatment-room images supplied for the website appear AI-generated, so keep those off the
   listing), and never before/after photos (NMC).
